Weather is recorded every 10 minutes for the 2020 whole year, which contains 21 meteorological indicators, such as air temperature, humidity, etc.
The input time series is processed into patches and fed to the LLM in a cross-modal interleaving manner: each raw patch representation is spliced together with its reprogrammed semantic counterpart in an alternating pattern, enabling the LLM to jointly perceive both numerical patterns and linguistic semantics.
